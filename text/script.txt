# The script of the game goes in this file.

# Declare characters used by this game. The color argument colorizes the
# name of the character.
define s = Character("Sword")
define k = Character("Knight")

# The game starts here.

label start:

	# Show a background. This uses a placeholder by default, but you can
	# add a file (named either "bg room.png" or "bg room.jpg") to the
	# images directory to show it.

	scene bg room

	# This shows a character sprite. A placeholder is used, but you can
	# replace it by adding a file named "sword.png" to the images
	# directory.
	show sword

	# These display lines of dialogue.
	k "I’m so tired..."
	s "Rough day?"
	k "Rough life. But yes, today has been ... eventful"
	s "Eventful - that's certainly a way to describe whatever happened to leave you here, covered in the blood of your enemies. And quite of a bit of your own blood too."
	s "And there's something new today, something delicious, something I haven't seen in you in a long time. Is that fear?"
	menu:
		"What do you fear?"
		"I fear the end":
			call fear_the_end
		"I fear my end":
			call fear_my_end
	s "Not me. I am but a tool. Used and discarded, but never truly able to end."
	k "All this talk about ends..."
	s "..."
	s "So, what’s the plan?"
	k "Plan?"
	s "Heroes always have a plan."
	menu: 
		"Heh. I’m all out.":
			call all_out
		"Heroes wouldn’t be hiding while the world burns.":
			call world_burn
		
	
	s "Well then..."
	s "A dead hero can’t save the world."
	k "It sounds like you’ve got a plan. Does that make you a hero?"
	s "Not a hero. But perhaps the tool to change fate."
	k "How do you mean that?"
	s "I am a legendary sword, wielded by heroes to alter the course of entire wars, even civilizations. My blade makes my wielder an unstoppable force, rendering ti"
	k "How lucky of me."
	
	return

label fear_the_end: 
	s "The end of what? The end of this battle? The end of this war? Your own end?"
	k "The end of this kingdom, and my people's dreams. Every passing moment I can feel their dreams being burned away like fireflies, no matter how I may clutch at them... they are surely lost the longer I sit here."
	k "Why am I even talking to you? When so much is being lost?"
	s "Yes, everything will end - unless you're willing to pay the right price."
	k "How long have you waited to try and strike this bargain? You don't ever stop trying do you? I suppose your wretched 'dreams' never end."
	return

label fear_my_end:
	s "Your end?"
	k "Our side was always fated to lose."
	s "Is fate not what you make of it?"
	k "Posh words for a thing made to bring death."
	return
	
label all_out:
	s "Oh. I see. So you’ve given up, right?"
	k "Given up? Don’t try to bait me, dear sword."
	s "Bait only works if there’s something there to catch. I simply meant to ask what you meant by 'all out'."
	k "I’m all out of ideas. All out of allies. All out of energy. All out of blood pretty soon. All out of..."
	s "..."
	k "I just wanted to see the moon one last time. Know the peace of night one last time. Perhaps have a conversation with a nonsensical sentient sword."
	s "What if this sentient sword had some ideas?"
	k "Ah, there’s the bait again. Last minute saviors are a thing of fairytales. Or perhaps nightmares?"
	s "One person’s fairytale is another person’s nightmare."
	k "Ah but both are dreams. Hmm... dreams..."
	s "There you go again, drifting away from our conversation. My first in many centuries actually. I feel like I deserve your full attention."
	k "Centuries? This castle has only been here for maybe a century. I was posted here 20 years ago and I've roamed every one of these halls and rooms. I've never seen you before. How is it we happen to meet and have this discussion right before the end?"
	s "People like bragging about doing things on their own. They're ashamed of the tools they had to use to get the job done. So I get hidden away a lot. Deep dungeons. Locked vaults. Faraway castles that no one cares about"
	k "Did the king bring you here?"
	s "Hard to tell when hidden away in a bag. But I remember fighting. A lot of fighting. A lot of cursing and crying. Then darkness."
	k 
	menu: 
		"Are you a cursed sword?":
			call cursed_sword
		"All of that sounds like bad news":
			call bad_news
	return


label cursed_sword:
	s "Cursed is a subjective term. Do I morph a human into something he never was before? No. Does my wielder have more choices they can make once they have me by hand? I'd like to think of myself as an opportunity maker"
	k "People can be terrible if given the incentive. But I'd not be naive enough to say people don't seek power and whatever gives them that power is a gift and a curse depending on who you are. My question has always been, to what extent can you ignore the consequences? I'm guessing you'd guarentee me a win tonight if I picked you up?"
	s "Consequences and guilt are not in my purview."
	k "You're not wrong. I'm sure every person who picked you up thought that they'd be able to control themselves. 20 years I've protected the people here. So many of them are lying dead out there. What will it mean to save the few remaining? There are supposed to be reinforcements coming but who knows when."
	s "Is there a minimum life count when it's worth the trouble?"
	k "Again, you confuse me for a fairytale hero. Heroes don't hide while the world burns." 
	#This end jumps into the world_burn label.
	return
label bad_news:
	k "And I've had bad news all day. I don't need to add something to my life that causes all that bad news. I've had regular ol' swords and that's terrible enough. And while I'm on the topic of bad news, where did they come from? Those caves have been empty for decades. There haven't even been bear or wolf attacks recently. Actually come to think of it, I can't remember the last time I got a report from the forest scouts about people being attacked by any animals. It used to be a regular thing."
	s ""
	return


label world_burn:
	s "TODO"
	return
